The lower stream passes through a sequence of shallow pools whose gravel beds retain water long after the surrounding fields have dried, providing a refuge for species that cannot tolerate rapid changes in temperature.

Sampling was conducted at regular intervals along the bank, with each location recorded against a fixed survey marker so that changes in the channel could be distinguished from differences in the position of the observer. Vegetation occasionally obscured the markers near the western bridge.

These observations establish a limited seasonal record rather than a complete description of the habitat, whose longer history would require comparable measurements from both wetter and substantially drier years.
